Understanding Loan Default

Loan default is when a borrower fails to make scheduled payments on a loan for an extended period. Income stability, existing debt load, and credit history are the usual drivers.

Impact: defaults raise losses for lenders and long-term borrowing costs for the applicant.

Screening role: the model surfaces patterns in historical applications to estimate default probability — useful for triage and research, not a final decision.

Predictions are for research and educational use. Consult a loan officer for actual lending decisions.
